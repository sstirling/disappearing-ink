{%- comment -%} Formats a date in AP style: months of six or more letters are abbreviated when they carry a specific date, the rest are spelled out. Strftime has no concept of this, so the months are listed and indexed by month number. Parameter: date (required). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign ap_months = "Jan.,Feb.,March,April,May,June,July,Aug.,Sept.,Oct.,Nov.,Dec." | split: "," -%} {%- assign month_index = include.date | date: "%-m" | times: 1 | minus: 1 -%} {{- ap_months[month_index] }} {{ include.date | date: "%-d, %Y" -}}
