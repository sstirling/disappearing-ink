Carol is a pseudonym for a loved one who I’m taking care of. She was diagnosed with Alzheimer’s disease on July 16, 2026. This is the story of that journey.

I’ve always processed big life events through creativity, and specifically writing. There are more people entering old age than at any time in American history. This is mostly for me, but I’m publishing it in hopes of connecting with the countless others starting this journey every day.

None of us are alone.
